Delete Your Yard Guard Data

Last updated: November 2025

Yard Guard is a privacy-first Android app. All your data is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server that stores your personal information. Nothing about your usage is uploaded to us, and we have no accounts, no cloud sync, and no analytics.

Data we store on your device

  • Your watched keywords
  • Your activity log (trigger history)
  • Your monitored apps list
  • Your Bluetooth speaker preference
  • Custom sound recordings (Pro users)
  • Captured camera notification images (when your camera app provides them)
  • Settings (theme, language, schedule, etc.)

How to delete all your data

Option 1: From inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Yard Guard
  2. Tap the Settings tab (bottom right)
  3. Scroll down to the ADVANCED section
  4. Tap Delete all data
  5. Confirm the dialog

This wipes all keywords, activity log, monitored apps, custom recordings, captured photos, and resets all settings to defaults. The app behaves as if freshly installed.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

  1. Long-press the Yard Guard icon on your home screen or app drawer
  2. Tap Uninstall (or drag to the Uninstall area)
  3. Confirm

Uninstalling removes the app and every piece of data associated with it from your device. Nothing remains.

Option 3: Clear app data via Android Settings

  1. Open Android Settings
  2. Go to Apps (or "Apps & notifications")
  3. Find and tap Yard Guard
  4. Tap Storage & cache
  5. Tap Clear storage (or "Clear data")

This wipes all app data without uninstalling the app.

What about server-side data?

There is none. We do not run a backend server that stores user information. The only outbound network requests Yard Guard makes are to api.open-meteo.com for weather data (only if you've granted location permission), and those requests do not include any account identifier or persistent user ID — just the latitude and longitude.

How long is data retained after deletion?

Immediately. The "Delete all data" option in-app, uninstalling, or clearing app data via Android Settings removes data immediately and permanently. There is no recovery and no retention period.
