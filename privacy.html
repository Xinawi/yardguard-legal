Yard Guard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

What we collect

Yard Guard collects only what's needed to function:

  • Notification content from camera apps you choose to monitor. Used locally to match against your keywords. Never transmitted.
  • Approximate location, only if you grant permission. Used only to fetch weather from open-meteo.com (so deterrents adapt to rain). Not shared with anyone else.
  • Activity log: trigger events stored locally on your device.
  • Audio recordings, only if you (Pro users) record custom sounds. Stored locally on your device.

What we don't collect

No personal info (name, email, contacts). No analytics SDKs. No ad networks. No tracking. No data shared with third parties except weather API.

Network requests

Yard Guard makes network requests to:

  • api.open-meteo.com — sends approximate latitude/longitude to receive weather data. Open-Meteo doesn't require accounts.

Your data, your control

All data stays on your device. To delete everything: Settings → Delete all data. Or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Yard Guard is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when policies change. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes.
